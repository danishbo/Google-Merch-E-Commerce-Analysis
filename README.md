# Background and Overview
The Google Merchandise Store is a global online retailer specialising in official Google-branded merchandise, including apparel, drinkware, lifestyle accessories, and tech items. Operating on an e-commerce platform, the store serves a global customer base across North America, EMEA, APAC, and LATAM. Key strategic priorities focus on evaluating conversion funnel efficiency, optimising digital marketing acquisition, driving customer retention, and improving order economics.

To support strategic decision-making in product planning, marketing, and user experience, historical clickstream event data was analysed. The key insights and recommendations focus on the following areas:

* **Funnel & User Journey Efficiency:** Quantified conversion rates and identified friction points from initial page view to order completion.
* **Marketing Channel Effectiveness:** Analysed acquisition sources (Direct, Organic, Paid Ads, Social, Email) to optimise marketing spend allocation.
* **Time to Purchase:** Measured customer decision velocity across different stages of the purchase path.
* **Sales & Revenue Performance:** Evaluated revenue dynamics, Average Order Value (AOV), and seasonal demand patterns.

# Executive Summary
The Google Merchandise Store generated $76K in total sales revenue across 708 completed orders over a 30-day active period, serving 4,268 unique visitors.

* **Overall visitor-to-buyer conversion stands at 16.59%,** with the largest drop-off occurring at the top of the funnel (View to Cart conversion at 31.21%). Once users initiate checkout, conversion strength remains high with over 80% progression to payment.
* Channel acquisition shows significant performance disparities: **Email marketing is the highest-performing driver with an overall conversion rate of 33.93%,** whereas Social Media yields only 6.70%. Converted buyers exhibit rapid decision velocity, taking an **average of 24.55 minutes from landing to purchase completion.**

# Summary of Insights
## Funnel & User Journey Efficiency
### Top-of-Funnel Drop-Off (View → Cart)
* Out of 4,268 unique site visitors, only 1,332 added items to their cart, resulting in a 31.21% View-to-Cart conversion rate. This represents the single **largest area of traffic drop-off.**
* This is the primary driver of lost revenue potential. While the site effectively attracts traffic, **product pages currently fail to convert initial interest into purchasing intent.**

### Bottom-of-Funnel Conversion Strength (Checkout → Purchase)
* Purchase Progression:
  * Cart to Checkout: 71.40% (951 users)
  * Checkout to Payment: 80.76% (768 users)
  * Payment to Purchase: 92.19% (708 users)
* The store achieves an overall visitor-to-buyer conversion rate of 16.59%. This indicates that the **checkout UX, payment gateway security, and checkout flow are highly effective and present minimal technical friction.**

## Marketing Channel Effectiveness
### Channel Dynamics
* Email Marketing:
  * Generates 33.93% overall conversion and an extraordinary 62.92% View-to-Cart rate.
  * **Email subscribers represent warm, highly engaged, or returning buyers.** Targeted promotional messaging and direct product links via email remove pre-purchase uncertainty and drive immediate buying action.

* Paid Ads vs. Organic Search:
  * Achieves a 21.10% overall conversion rate, outperforming Organic Search (17.14%).
  * **Paid ads bring in visitors who are ready to buy,** resulting in a higher conversion rate (21%). **Organic search brings in the most overall visitors (1,750),** but fewer of them purchase (17%).

* Social Media:
  * Brings in substantial traffic (1,253 visitors) but suffers from an abysmal 13.65% View-to-Cart rate and 6.70% overall conversion.
  * **Social media users are predominantly casual browsers exhibiting low immediate purchase intent.** Directing social ad traffic directly to standard catalog pages results in high bounce rates.

## Time to Purchase
* Converted buyers spend an **average of 24.55 minutes from their first page view to completing their purchase.**
* Time Breakdown:
  * View to Cart: 11.19 minutes (Time spent evaluating product specifications, pricing, and options).
  * Cart to Purchase: 13.36 minutes (Time spent reviewing order summary, entering shipping details, and completing payment).
* The short ~25-minute timeline shows that **purchases are fast, impulse-driven decisions rather than long research processes.**
* Any friction during this brief window like unexpected shipping costs, slow page loads, mandatory account creation, or complex checkout forms, may lead to lost sales.

## Sales & Revenue Performance
### Key Metrics
* **30-Day Revenue:** $76,037.93.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $107.40.
* **Revenue Per Buyer (RPB):** $107.40.
* **Revenue Per Visitor (RPV):** $17.82.

- **Each buyer placed 1 order during this 30-day period.**
- Because every visitor generates an average of $17.82, our **advertising cost per click (CPC) must stay well below $17.82 to remain profitable.**

# Recommendations
**Merchandising & Product Experience Team - Optimize Top-of-Funnel Conversion**
* Address the 68.79% drop-off between page views and cart additions by optimizing product display pages with clearer call-to-action buttons, dynamic stock counters, and prominent customer reviews.
* Introduce pre-configured product bundles and threshold-based incentives (such as "Add $15 more for free shipping") to increase Average Order Value above the current $107.40 benchmark.

**Digital Marketing Team - Reallocate Acquisition Budgets**
* Double down on email marketing and CRM lifecycle sequences, which currently yield a high 33.93% overall conversion rate.
* Revamp social media targeting and landing page experiences to resolve the low 13.65% View-to-Cart rate, focusing social traffic on high-converting impulse items rather than broad catalog pages.
* Benchmark target Cost Per Click (CPC) against the $17.82 Revenue Per Visitor (RPV) metric to ensure positive return on ad spend across paid acquisition channels.

**UI/UX & Operations Team - Leverage Conversion Speed**
* Implement real-time engagement tactics (such as exit-intent popups and guest checkout options) within the 24.55-minute user decision window to prevent session abandonments.
* Maintain the high 80%+ checkout-to-payment retention by offering express payment methods like Google Pay and Apple Pay.
* Allow first-time buyers to complete purchases without mandatory account creation to prevent friction during the critical 13.36-minute checkout window.

# Appendix
## Assumptions and Caveats
* Sales data is complete and accurate with no missing or duplicate transactions.
* Marketing channel attribution is based on last-touch or final-click data.
* External factors (e.g., COVID-19, inflation, supply chain issues) may have impacted customer behavior and sales but are not modeled directly.
* Profitability was not assessed due to lack of cost or margin data; analysis is based on revenue only.
* Customer-level segmentation (e.g., demographics, behavior) was not possible due to limited customer data.
